ABOUT TAMIL NADU's FAMOUS PLACES

Tamilnadu is a beautiful state situated in the southern part of india.It is famous for its colorful festivals and also for its delicious and traditional food

YARCAUD

Yercaud is a peaceful hill station in the Shevaroy Hills of the Eastern Ghats in Tamil Nadu, famed for its cool climate, vast coffee plantations, and evergreen forests.

KANYAKUMARI

Kanyakumari is the southernmost town on the mainland of India, located in the state of Tamil Nadu.Historically known as Cape Comorin, this coastal gem is famous as the unique spot where three major bodies of water meet—the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal, and the Indian Ocean. It is one of the rare places in the world where you can watch both the sunrise and the sunset over the water from the same shore.

MADURAI

Madurai is an ancient and vibrant city situated on the banks of the Vaigai River in the south-central region of Tamil Nadu, India.

RAMESWARAM

Rameswaram is a sacred island town in the Ramanathapuram district of Tamil Nadu, located at the tip of the Indian peninsula on Pamban Island

OOTY

Ooty, officially known as Udhagamandalam, is a famous hill station in the Nilgiri district of Tamil Nadu.Nestled in the Western Ghats at an altitude of 2,240 meters, it is widely known as the "Queen of Hill Stations". The British developed the town as a summer retreat to escape the intense heat of the plains, leaving behind colonial landmarks like St. Stephen’s Church and charming stone cottages. Today, tourists flock to Ooty to experience its cool climate, misty mornings, and rolling hills blanketed with emerald-green tea gardens.

MAHABALI TOURIST

(officially Mamallapuram) is a historic coastal town in Tamil Nadu, India, famous for its 7th- and 8th-century rock-cut Hindu monuments.
